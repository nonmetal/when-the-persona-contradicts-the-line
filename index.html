In one example

Same words, two personas. The stock route often misses the turn the persona calls for; a learned interface between plan and synthesizer recovers much of it.

Listen

Sixteen test items, drawn at random. Base is the stock module; Bypassed passes the planner's own vector; Supervised fine-tunes the module; Learned maps the phrase to the emotion latent. All four receive the planner's duration factor.

✓ the measured change from A to B has the direction the item declares. Failures are shown as they fall; the example above is one of these items.
